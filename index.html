The Psychology of Money

NICHE: FINANCE

LONG-FORM
TARGET AUDIENCE:Young professionals
& investors (18–35)

OLD SCRIPT

Money is an important part of everyone’s life. We all work hard to earn money, save money, and build a better future.
But managing money is not always as simple as earning more.
In this video, we’ll understand the psychology behind money, why people make certain financial decisions, and how we can manage our money better.

MY NEW SCRIPT

You can earn more money and still end up with less wealth. Because money isn't just a numbers game. It's a behavior game. The way you think about spending, saving, risk, and investing can completely change the financial decisions you make.
And here's the interesting part: some of our worst money decisions don't feel wrong when we're making them.
So why do we behave this way? Why does earning more often lead to spending more? And why can two people with the same income end up with completely different financial lives?

WHAT I IMPROVED

Stronger curiosity-driven hook
Clearer angle on “psychology”

More story-driven opening
Better flow & depth

How I Study for 10+ Hours

NICHE: EDUCATION

LONG-FORM
TARGET AUDIENCE:Students & exam
aspirants (15–25)

OLD SCRIPT

Studying for long hours can be difficult, especially when you have a lot of syllabus to complete and exams are getting closer.
In this video, I'll share how I study for more than 10 hours a day, how I manage my time, stay focused, and complete my daily study targets.
If you're a student or preparing for an important exam, this video will help you build a better study routine and make your study hours more productive.

MY NEW SCRIPT

Studying for 10+ hours sounds impossible until you realize that the goal isn't to study longer, but to study better.
I used to struggle with long study sessions too. I'd spend hours at my desk, but still feel like I hadn't done enough.
So what changed?
I changed the way I planned my day, managed my focus, and studied difficult subjects. In this video, I'll show you my 10 hour study routine and how you can make your study hours actually count.

WHAT I IMPROVED

Personal angle + story for connection
Specific promise in the opening

More depth & clarity of value
Smoother transition & flow
